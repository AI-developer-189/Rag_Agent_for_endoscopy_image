# Endoscopy Clinical Guidelines - General Overview

## Colonoscopy Preparation
- **Bowel preparation**: PEG electrolyte solution or split-dose preparation
- **Quality metric**: Boston Bowel Preparation Scale (BBPS) score ≥2 per segment
- **Cecal intubation rate target**: ≥95%
- **Withdrawal time**: ≥6 minutes for optimal polyp detection

## Upper Endoscopy Guidelines
- **Indications**: Dysphagia, odynophagia, GI bleeding, anemia, unexplained weight loss
- **Biopsy protocol**: Systematic antrectomy biopsy for H. pylori; gastric/duodenal biopsies for dysplasia in Barrett's
- **Sedation guidelines**: Use lowest effective dose; monitor cardiopulmonary function
- **Post-procedure**: NPO until gag reflex returns; discharge criteria met

## Endoscopic Hemostasis
- **First-line**: Clipping, thermal coagulation, injection therapy
- **Adrenaline (1:10,000) injection** followed by thermal or mechanical therapy
- **Clips**: First-line for ulcer bleeding; effective for post-polypectomy bleeding
- **Surgery consideration**: Failure of endoscopic hemostasis, ongoing hemodynamic instability

## Polypectomy Standards
- **Cold snare**: Polyps <10mm, tubular adenomas
- **Cold biopsy**: Polyps <5mm, random sampling
- **Hot snare**: Polyps 10-20mm with appropriate endpoint technique
- **EMR**: Sessile polyps >20mm, laterally spreading lesions
- **Histopathology**: Required for all polyps >1mm

## Adenoma Detection Quality Metrics
- **ADR**: ≥25% average (men), ≥15% average (women) across endoscopists
- **Per cecum ADR**: Report separately for each colonoscopy
- **Mean number of adenomas per colonoscopy**: Benchmark auditing
- **Serrated polyp detection rate**: Emerging quality metric

## Post-Endoscopy Care
- **Discharge criteria**: Stable vital signs, able to tolerate oral intake, gag reflex present
- **Post-polypectomy precautions**: Avoid heavy lifting, no aspirin/NSAIDs for 48-72 hours (per protocol)
- **Follow-up scheduling**: Based on adenoma histology, number, size, and surveillance interval
- **Red flags**: Severe abdominal pain, fever, rectal bleeding >1 hour post-procedure
